Septica Game Rules

Game Overview

Septica is a trick-taking card game for 4 players arranged in 2 teams. The goal is to be the first team to reach a certain number of points by collecting scoring cards (10s and Aces) during hands.

Setup

Team Purple

Player 1 & Player 3

Team Black

Player 2 & Player 4

Each player starts with 4 cards. New cards are dealt after each hand until the deck is empty.

Scoring Cards

10s and Aces

Each 10 and Ace is worth 1 point

Other Cards

7, 8, 9, J, Q, K are worth 0 points

Cut Cards

Special Cards that Change Hand Ownership
  • All 7s - Always cut regardless of the current hand
  • Matching Value - Any card with the same value as the first card played in the hand

Example: If the first card played is a King of Hearts, then all 7s and all Kings can cut and take ownership of the hand.

How to Play

Playing Cards
  1. Players take turns playing one card each
  2. The first card played sets the "cut value" for that hand
  3. Each subsequent card is played by the next player in turn
  4. After 4 cards are played, if it's the starter's turn again, they must make a decision
Starter Decision

When play returns to the hand starter, they have three options:

Take Hand

Only available if the starter currently owns the hand

Forfeit Hand

Give up the hand to whoever currently owns it

Continue

Play a cut card to continue the hand and take ownership

Hand Resolution
  • The player who owns the hand when it ends gets all the points from cards in that hand
  • The hand owner becomes the starter for the next hand
  • New cards are dealt to all players (if available)

Winning & Scoring

Regular Win

Team with more points at game end gets 1 lobby point

Perfect Game

If losing team wins 0 hands, winning team gets 2 lobby points

Important Note

A perfect game (2 points) is awarded only when the losing team wins zero hands during the entire game, not just when the final score is 8-0. A team can lose 8-0 but still get only 1 point awarded to the opponent if they won at least one hand with non-scoring cards.

Tie Games

If both teams end with the same score, the next game will be worth 2 lobby points instead of 1.

Starting Player Rotation

The starting player rotates fairly between all players:

  1. Game 1: Team Purple, Player 1
  2. Game 2: Team Black, Player 1
  3. Game 3: Team Purple, Player 2
  4. Game 4: Team Black, Player 2
  5. Game 5: Cycle repeats...

Strategy Tips

Offense
  • Save cut cards for crucial moments
  • Try to take hands with high-value cards
  • Coordinate with your teammate
Defense
  • Block opponents from taking valuable hands
  • Force difficult decisions on opponents
  • Watch for perfect game situations
