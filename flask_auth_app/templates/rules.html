Septica Game Rules

Game Overview

Septica is a trick-taking card game for 4 players arranged in 2 teams. The goal is to collect the most scoring cards (10s and Aces) across hands.

Setup

Team Purple

Player 1 & Player 3 (seats 0 and 2)

Team Black

Player 2 & Player 4 (seats 1 and 3)

  • Full 32-card deck — 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K, A in all four suits
  • Each player starts with 4 cards. One new card is dealt per player after each hand until the deck runs out.

Scoring Cards

10s and Aces

Each 10 and Ace is worth 1 point (8 points total in the deck).

Other Cards

7, 8, 9, J, Q, K are worth 0 points.

Cut Cards

Cards that transfer hand ownership
  • All 7s — always cut, regardless of what was played first
  • Matching value — any card with the same value as the first card of the hand

Example: If the first card is K♥, then all 7s and all Kings cut and take ownership.

How to Play

Playing Cards
  1. Players take turns playing one card each.
  2. The first card sets the cut value for the hand.
  3. After all 4 players have played, the starter must make a decision.
Starter Decision

When play returns to the starter, they choose one of:

Take Hand

Only available if the starter currently owns the hand.

Forfeit Hand

Give the hand to whoever currently owns it.

Continue

Play a cut card to reclaim ownership and keep the hand going.

Hand Resolution
  • The hand owner's team scores all points from the cards in that hand.
  • The hand owner becomes the starter for the next hand.
  • One new card is dealt to each player (if the deck is not empty).

Winning & Lobby Scoring

Regular Win

Team with more points gets 1 lobby point.

Perfect Game

If the losing team wins zero hands, the winner gets 2 lobby points (bonus for shutout).

Ties

If both teams finish with equal points, the next game is worth 2 lobby points instead of 1.

Starting Player Rotation

  1. Game 1: Team Purple, Player 1
  2. Game 2: Team Black, Player 1
  3. Game 3: Team Purple, Player 2
  4. Game 4: Team Black, Player 2
  5. Game 5+: Cycle repeats…

Strategy Tips

Offense
  • Save 7s for hands that contain 10s or Aces.
  • Coordinate with your teammate — let them take point-rich hands if they own them.
Defense
  • Cut opponents' valuable hands with a 7 to deny them points.
  • Watch for perfect-game opportunities — shutting out the opponent earns a bonus point.

Game Overview

The 3-player variant is a free-for-all — every player competes individually. There are no teams. The player who accumulates the most points across all hands wins.

Setup

Modified Deck — 30 Cards

Two eights are removed from the deck before play:

  • 8♥ (Eight of Hearts) — removed
  • 8♦ (Eight of Diamonds) — removed
Wild Eights

The remaining two eights become wild cut cards — they act exactly like 7s and can cut any hand:

  • 8♣ (Eight of Clubs) — wild
  • 8♠ (Eight of Spades) — wild

Each player starts with 4 cards. One new card is dealt per player after each hand until the deck runs out.

Scoring Cards

10s and Aces

Each 10 and Ace is worth 1 point (8 points total in the deck).

Other Cards

7, 9, J, Q, K are worth 0 points. The 8♣ and 8♠ are also 0 points (but are powerful cut cards).

Cut Cards

Cards that transfer hand ownership
  • All 7s — always cut
  • 8♣ and 8♠ — wild eights, always cut (like a 7)
  • Matching value — any card with the same value as the first card of the hand

Example: If the first card is K♠, then all 7s, 8♣, 8♠, and all Kings cut and take ownership.

How to Play

Playing Cards
  1. Players take turns playing one card each (3 cards per round).
  2. The first card sets the cut value for the hand.
  3. After all 3 players have played, the starter makes a decision.
Starter Decision

When play returns to the starter, they choose one of:

Take Hand

Only available if the starter currently owns the hand.

Forfeit Hand

Give the hand to whoever currently owns it.

Continue

Play a cut card (7, 8♣, 8♠, or matching value) to reclaim ownership and extend the hand.

Hand Resolution
  • The hand owner scores all points from the cards in that hand for themselves (no team sharing).
  • The hand owner becomes the starter for the next hand.
  • One new card is dealt to each player (if the deck is not empty).

Winning & Lobby Scoring

Regular Win

The player with the most points gets 1 lobby point.

Perfect Game

If the winner takes every single hand (both opponents won zero hands), they get 2 lobby points.

Ties

If two or more players finish with equal points, no lobby point is awarded and the next game is worth 2 lobby points.

Starting Player Rotation

The starting player rotates fairly among all three players:

  1. Game 1: Player 1
  2. Game 2: Player 2
  3. Game 3: Player 3
  4. Game 4+: Cycle repeats…

Strategy Tips

Offense
  • The wild 8s (8♣, 8♠) are extremely powerful — save them for hands loaded with 10s and Aces.
  • As the starter, timing when to take vs. continue is critical since no teammate will help you.
Defense
  • In a 3-player game you have two opponents — decide which one to block when both threaten to steal a valuable hand.
  • Forfeiting a cheap hand can be a good tactical move to set up a better position next hand.
